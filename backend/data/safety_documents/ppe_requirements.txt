# PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) MANDATORY STANDARD
Document ID: SOP-LAB-PPE-004
Revision: 5.0 | Classification: OSHA 1910.132 / Biosafety Laboratory Standard

1. CORE PPE REQUIREMENTS FOR LABORATORY ENTRY
Whenever entering an active chemical, biological, or analytical laboratory, all personnel (researchers, technicians, students, visitors) must don the foundational "Four Pillars of PPE":

A. LAB COAT (Class 1 / Class 2 Certified):
- Required: 100% white cotton or flame-resistant treated Nomex/polyester blend knee-length coat with snap or button fasteners.
- Fastening: Lab coat must remain fully buttoned up from top to bottom at all times while in the lab. Open/unbuttoned lab coats do not protect chest or torso and pose entanglement hazards.
- Sleeves: Wrist cuffs should be elasticized or tailored to prevent dragging into chemical containers or Bunsen burners.
- Decontamination: Never wash contaminated lab coats at home. Use professional facility laundry services.

B. SAFETY GOGGLES & EYE PROTECTION (ANSI Z87.1 D3 Certified):
- Required: Indirectly vented chemical splash goggles featuring airtight or baffled seals around the eye sockets.
- Standard prescription eyeglasses or sunglasses DO NOT provide impact or chemical splash protection.
- When working with high splash hazards, liquid nitrogen, or concentrated acids, add a full polycarbonate face shield OVER the goggles.
- UV or Laser Operations: Require specialized wavelength-specific laser safety eyewear matched to the optical density (OD) of the beam.

C. PROTECTIVE GLOVES:
- Required: Nitrile gloves (minimum 4-mil thickness) for general bench chemistry and biological protection.
- Material Compatibility:
  * Nitrile: Excellent for weak acids, bases, biological samples, and incidental solvent contact.
  * Neoprene / Butyl: Required for handling aggressive polar solvents (acetone, ketones, DMF, ethers) and concentrated hydrofluoric acid.
  * Cryogenic Gloves: Thermal insulated gloves for handling liquid nitrogen dewars.
  * Heat Resistant: Heavy Kevlar/silicone mitts for autoclaves, muffles, and high-temp ovens.
- Proper Glove Removal (Doffing): Pinch outside near cuff, peel down inside-out, ball up inside second hand, slide clean finger under second cuff, roll into neat inverted pouch, dispose in solid waste. Never touch doorknobs or phones with gloved hands.

D. RESPIRATORY PROTECTION & MASKS (OSHA 1910.134):
- Particulate Masks: N95/FFP2 respirators required when handling hazardous powders, crystalline silica, aerosolized biological agents, or airborne allergens.
- Chemical Vapor Cartridge Respirators (Half-mask / Full-face): Required if chemical vapors exceed permissible exposure limits (PEL) outside certified chemical fume hoods.
- Surgical / Procedure masks: Provide droplet containment in biological containment level 2 (BSL-2) areas.

2. ATTIRE & FOOTWEAR RESTRICTIONS
- Legs: Must be completely covered by sturdy long pants or ankle-length trousers. Shorts, skirts, culottes, and leggings/tights are strictly forbidden.
- Footwear: Fully closed-toe and closed-heel leather or solid non-permeable synthetic shoes. Sandals, flip-flops, crocs with holes, mesh sneakers, and high heels are strictly forbidden.
- Hair: Long hair (touching collar or longer) must be securely tied back into a bun or ponytail.
- Jewelry: Dangling chains, loose rings, and wristbands must be removed to avoid snagging and chemical entrapment.

3. PPE COMPLIANCE & RISK GRADING
- 100% Compliance (Coat + Goggles + Gloves + Mask when required): LOW RISK (Status: Safe & Compliant)
- Missing 1 item (e.g. gloves or mask in non-volatile area): MEDIUM RISK (Warning issued)
- Missing 2 items (e.g. goggles and gloves): HIGH RISK (Work halted immediately)
- Missing Lab Coat and Goggles or multiple personnel unequipped: CRITICAL RISK (Mandatory safety stand-down)
